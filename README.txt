HP PRINTER HELP GUIDES WEBSITE

Includes homepage, searchable article directory, support page, 100 individual HTML articles, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

Publishing:
1. Upload the contents of this folder to your hosting root.
2. Replace https://your-hpprinter-domain.example in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with your actual published domain.
3. Keep the articles folder and relative paths intact.

Header:
The navbar has Home and Articles text links, plus exactly two 3D-styled buttons: Set Up and Support. Both buttons show on-page guidance and intentionally have no external URL or redirect.

Content:
Each article is an independent educational guide. HP printer menus and features vary by model. Verify model-specific instructions through HP's official support resources. This website is not affiliated with HP Inc.
